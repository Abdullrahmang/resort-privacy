Privacy Policy — Resort Booking System

Last updated: October 21, 2026

This policy explains how the Resort Booking System handles information when customers contact us through WhatsApp to ask about or make caravan reservations.

Information We May Collect

We may collect your name, phone number, requested reservation date, start time, end time, and selected caravan.

How We Use Information

We use this information to check availability, create and manage reservations, prevent booking conflicts, and communicate with customers about their bookings.

WhatsApp and AI

Messages sent through WhatsApp may be processed by automated software or AI to help answer questions and collect reservation details. WhatsApp and any AI service providers involved may process information according to their own privacy policies.

Sharing and Retention

We do not intend to sell customers' personal information. Reservation information may be stored in our booking system and retained as needed to manage reservations and meet applicable legal requirements. Access should be limited to authorized people.

Data Deletion Requests

To request access to or deletion of your personal information, contact the Resort Booking System operator through the official contact channel used to arrange your reservation. Please include your phone number and enough information to identify the relevant reservation. We may need to verify your identity before processing a request.

Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact the Resort Booking System operator through the official contact channel used to arrange your reservation.
