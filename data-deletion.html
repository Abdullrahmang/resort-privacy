Data Deletion Instructions

If you have made a reservation through the Resort Booking System and want to request deletion of your personal information, please contact us through WhatsApp.

How to Request Deletion

  1. Send us a WhatsApp message requesting deletion of your personal information.
  2. Include your name, the phone number used for the reservation, and enough details to identify the booking.
  3. We may ask you to verify your identity before processing the request.
  4. We will review your request and explain if any information must be retained for legal or legitimate business reasons.
